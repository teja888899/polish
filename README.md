## Polish

Polish turns a rough message into a professional one, right where you typed it.

## The problem

Writing to a professor usually goes like this: write a draft, copy it into ChatGPT or Claude, ask it to make the draft professional, then copy the result back into your email or Discord. That's too many steps, especially on a phone.

## What we're building

A small app for your laptop:

1. Type your message anywhere (Gmail, Outlook, Discord…)
2. Select it and press a shortcut
3. The message is rewritten in place, polite and professional

You read it and send it yourself. Polish never sends anything for you.

Once the laptop version works, we'll make a phone version.

## Who's doing what


Person 1: The laptop app: the shortcut, grabbing your text, and putting the new text back 
Person 2: The AI part: turning a rough draft into a professional message
